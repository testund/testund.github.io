This series breaks the endless repetitions of the Mechanism series with an abrupt discontinuity.

The short, roughly 60 second, movies are like infomercial breaks for death.

The five breaks feature the agents of death — other, self, body, error, and nature — powered by the elements time, earth, air, water, and fire.
