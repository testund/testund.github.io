Fault, mixed media, 2012, figure about 4” tall
(background video recorded on the San Andreas fault,
Los Trancos Open Space Preserve, Palo Alto, CA)

Sleep above the lake of fire,
Oblivious the basic fault,
Rock a boy by milky hammock,
Open mouth two blind eyed sloth,
Second death cross over magma,
Rent the veil and swallow up.
Oh no…
