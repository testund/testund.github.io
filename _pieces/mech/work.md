Repeating:
every night sweep up the same floor,
looking at the clock.
No one knows
this is a performance art work,
looking for the next paycheck,
Till the time to
wait in line to punch the time card,
race out of the parking lot.
It’s too
bright and noisy to sleep all day.
Get up. Go back to work.
Repeating:
every day work on the same stuff,
passing time away.
No one knows
this is an important art work,
say it doesn’t pay to play,
Till the time to
wait to sink into a stupor,
rise up, stumble off to quit.
It’s too
dark and anxious to sleep all night.
Get up. Go back to work.

Work, mixed media, 2011, figure about 6” H standing up.
