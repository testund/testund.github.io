Freefall: 
Pleasant Lake Maine, Stroke Anniversary 

torches pass from crown to crown 
sparing the softwoods thick on the crest 
in a slow-motion months-long smolder. 
calendar pages three-quarters turned over. 
backdrop blue-screen sky beyond green 
spruce hemlock and pine 
pops the warm primaries yellow red 
dry bristle brushed  
maple beech and birch cold mirrored 
in becalmed morning water. 
oak lags and browns at last. 
summer people close up camps, 
stow kayaks barbeques Adirondack chairs, 
bank docks with dead bug legs on rocks 
before the freeze-dried fireworks. 

green steel burns under an acetylene tip 
cutting blue voids in an instant replay 
of red yellow spark shower cascade.  
count three score years and maybe twenty yet. 
the trace memory afterimage floats 
branching wrinkled purple turquoise 
behind crows feet eyelid afternoon, 
the translucent sheath over pale bone,  
colorless haze of hair and faint veins, 
eroded earlobes, twitch, slump, wheeze. 
red atones for the stained glass. 
November people hunker down, 
splatter watercolor brown melanoma, 
and prop bent limbs with quad canes  
when fireflies swarm in acid-etched amber. 

they envy the oxide hues of dying leaves, 
bank ashen embers orange when breathed. 
their freefall cremation exhumes in mulch, 
with time-lapsed sodium sparklers overexposed. 
the old immolate with grace as they fade from life 
and sponge clouds with day-glow lacquer flames. 
high cirrus sunset foliage tags the red orange sky, 
while the bloodless cumulous cortex bleaches white.
