Stammer Versus Verbal Apraxia, The Colossal Conflict. The Sequel to 

King Kong Versus Godzilla, The Two Mightiest Monsters of All Time. 

Drum Beats. Dumb beasts. Kronng! Rooar! 

Bully classmate on my playground stomach laughs “You talk like Porky Pig!” 

ridiculous crazy retard, that’s all folks, shame leaves me speechless. 

Versus: 

Coiffed caseworker hospital bedside enunciates loud and slow, classifying me  

as foreign demented children, anger makes me fluent, “I’m mute not deaf!” 

Gleaming needle gripped between thumb and pointer with thinner thread 

grasped between the other pair of pads, I pass the indistinct end against 

the invisible eye, hunched, gut clenched, breath tensed, squinting 

tighter at each missed fro and retry to, avoiding eye contact. 

Versus: 

Old barn maw, motes churn in sunblades cut from bark-edge planks, 

I pitch armfuls of crisp straw from mounds as high as the cross beams,   

with a vague urgency for a cold hard smooth sound which might not exist at all. 

Grouped with various amusements past the snack bar near the restrooms 

opposite the bowling lanes, a claw crane game holds my fingers fast 

against its joystick, with a plush phoneme plucked from the multicolored heap 

of audible stuffies caught in its chrome teeth, but when the trigger clicks, 

the soft partial syllable swings suspended on the cable, back and forth,  

slowly, in silence, leaking spittle, on the other side of the glass. 

Versus: 

 
 
  
The claw comes away empty, with nothing to speak of from the jumble 

of letters layered at the bottom of the glazed cube, and hovers expectantly 

above the round outlet hole.  

On the uphill dirt road after a November rain, the left rear wheel 

fishtails into a shallow ditch and spins free in the mud. 

I shift into reverse and ease out the clutch, and the car lurches, but 

digs deeper, spraying the floorpan with slurry, again forward 

in second, hunching me into the seatback, and showering    

the underbrush viscous brown soup in the rearview, while excavating the rut, 

again reverse tire whine, again forward earthen surge, repeat 

reverse, forward, reverse 

Versus: 

Around a downhill curve, on a winter highbeam blacktop, the steering wheel 

decouples, the hood comes around to light up the berm, the brake pedal 

bottoms out near the mat, the windshield steadycams to pan backwards  

at full speed behind, a whirling teacup carnival ride spins round, 

and I pirouette on black ice. 

I tauten the T-handle in my palm, arm extended straight from my squat, 

instep braced against the mower housing, and pull. 

The rotor turns, the piston recoils, a whisp of smoke escapes  

the muffler, a pause, then the spring chirps as the slack cord rewinds, 

as I crouch back bent to try again, while  

the grass greens the smell of gasoline. 

Versus: 

A yard of rope swings from my outstretched hand, seeking something 

 
 
to tie onto, or maybe leashing an unseen seeing ear dog which trees 

the sound which starts the word I want to say. 

Leaping boulders in Little Sandy Creek early August low water, 

looking ahead in the stream of consciousness 

while teetering from word to word, watching out for footfalls 

too steep or slippery or small or too long a stride, 

often stuck between a hard rock and a sudsy pool or tortured channel, 

then backtracking for a synonym or an alternative phrase or 

a whole other idea entirely,    

planning a path to what I intend across the smooth stones 

which double time my feet in the standing still sun 

versus French kiss my tongue with their sparkling grit.  

Some say everything happens for reason, including my stroke, 

I don’t think I believe that, 

but in the climatic scene at the bottom of the Pacific ocean 

Godzilla defeated King Kong, at least 

in the Japanese version.
